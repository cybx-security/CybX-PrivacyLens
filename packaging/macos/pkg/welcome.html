Welcome to PrivacyLens @VERSION@

PrivacyLens finds personal information — Social Security numbers, card numbers, medical and bank details — stored in files on this Mac, and shows you exactly where it is.

This installer will:

  • install PrivacyLens for everyone who uses this Mac,
  • add it to the Applications folder,
  • schedule an automatic scan every Sunday at 2:00 AM.

Nothing PrivacyLens finds ever leaves this Mac.
